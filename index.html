INTERNSHIP AGREEMENT

This Internship Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of acceptance, by and between the Host Company and the Intern.

1. TERM & SCHEDULE
The internship engagement will commence on August 1st and will continue for a term of three (3) months.

2. TERMINATION & NOTICE
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time. However, the Intern shall provide a minimum of ninety (90) days notice period prior to early resignation. Failure to provide ninety days notice will forfeit stipend milestones.

3. RENEWAL
This internship is subject to automatic renewal at the end of the term for another three months under identical terms, unless notification is given.

4. STIPEND REFUND
The Intern is entitled to a full refund of any travel expenses within thirty (30) days of expense submission.
