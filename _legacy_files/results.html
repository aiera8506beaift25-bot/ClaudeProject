TechCorp Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

INTERNSHIP OFFER LETTER

Date: June 12, 2026

Dear Arjun Mehta,

We are pleased to extend this offer of internship to you at TechCorp Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company"). This letter details the terms and conditions governing your internship. Please read all clauses carefully before signing.

1. POSITION AND DURATION

You will be joining as a Software Development Intern in the Engineering division reporting to the Head of Engineering. The internship shall commence on July 1, 2026, and continue for a period of three (3) months, ending on September 30, 2026, unless extended or terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein.

2. COMPENSATION

The intern shall receive a monthly stipend of ₹8,000 (Rupees Eight Thousand only). This stipend is subject to applicable tax deductions as per prevailing government regulations. The Company reserves the right to revise, reduce, or withhold the stipend based on attendance and performance metrics determined solely at the Company's discretion. Payment will be credited on or before the 7th of each following month.

3. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

Working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. The intern may be required to work on weekends, public holidays, or beyond regular working hours as deemed necessary by the management without any additional compensation or overtime pay whatsoever.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

The intern agrees to maintain absolute confidentiality regarding all proprietary information, trade secrets, business strategies, client lists, and technical data. This confidentiality obligation shall remain in full force and effect indefinitely — even after termination of this internship — and shall apply across the globe without any territorial limitation. Breach of this clause shall make the intern liable to the Company for damages and legal action.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All inventions, works, developments, discoveries, improvements, software code, designs, and creative works made, conceived, or contributed to by the intern during the internship — whether or not related to the Company's business and whether or not developed during working hours or using Company resources — shall be the exclusive property of the Company. The intern agrees to execute any documents required to transfer such rights.

6. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION

For a period of 12 (twelve) months following the conclusion of this internship, the intern shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, be employed by, consult for, or have a financial interest in any business that is in direct or indirect competition with the Company's business operations anywhere in India.

7. TERMINATION

The Company may terminate this internship at any time, with or without cause, by providing 7 (seven) days prior written notice. The intern is required to provide 30 (thirty) days written notice prior to resignation. All Company property, including access credentials, must be returned upon termination.

8. CERTIFICATE OF INTERNSHIP

Upon successful completion of the internship, the Company shall provide an experience certificate within 45 (forty-five) business days. Issuance of the certificate is contingent upon satisfactory performance as determined solely by the Company and return of all Company assets.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Bangalore, Karnataka.

By signing below, you accept all terms and conditions of this offer.

Authorised Signatory – TechCorp Solutions

Legal Glossary

Key Legal Terms Explained

Plain-English definitions for terms used in this document.

Non-Compete Clause

A clause that restricts you from working at competitor companies for a set period after leaving. Can limit your career options significantly.

Intellectual Property (IP)

Legal ownership of your creative work — code, designs, inventions. An IP clause can transfer ownership of your creations to the company.

Confidentiality (NDA)

An agreement to keep business information private. Broad or indefinite NDAs can prevent you from freely discussing your experience.

Notice Period

The time you or the employer must give before ending an agreement. Unequal notice periods favour the employer and disadvantage you.

Stipend Deduction

Withholding or reducing your pay. A "discretionary" deduction clause gives the company unchecked power over your compensation.

Governing Law

The legal jurisdiction that applies to the contract. Disputes must be resolved in that city's courts — which may be far from where you live.
